Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · QuattroMondi Publisher

1. This landing page

This page collects no personal data: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels. Outgoing links simply take you to Pinterest profiles or, in the future, to product pages and newsletter signup forms (each clearly labelled, including affiliate links marked as advertising).

2. The QuattroMondi Publisher app

The app is a single-user, personal scheduler: it publishes the owner's own pins to the owner's own Pinterest business accounts through the official Pinterest API (OAuth 2.0). Pinterest account data (boards, pins) is accessed only with the account owner's explicit consent and only to create, schedule and measure the owner's own content.

3. Where data lives

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose.
